Show the example that belongs to the gloss, and recover derivations stated late

Two defects, both visible in the lesson view.

## 1. An example illustrated the wrong sense

The card for e shows the gloss and, as a word means several things, an example
sentence. firstExample() scanned every sense and returned the first example it
found, so the headline conjunction sense (and) was illustrated by a sentence
belonging to a different, prepositional sense (of, + dative). The sentence
contained no and at all, which made the card look broken rather than merely
imprecise.

The example now comes from the sense whose gloss is on screen, and the
fallback to another sense is limited to the same part of speech. A conjunction
gloss with no conjunction example is better shown without an example than
illustrated by a preposition sentence. e now shows and and no example.

tools/test_example_pairing.mjs covers it, including a check that no card anywhere
in the deck shows a cross-POS example, and a negative control.

## 2. esell was split from its real root

esell (sober, on an empty stomach) really is built from e-, a privative prefix,
plus sille. The build only read the first sentence of the etymology, and esell
names its base in the third: Interpretible as e- + sille.

Every sentence is now scanned, but a comparison is not a derivation. nuk holds
the aside typologically compare Latin non (not) ... (< ne + unus ~ unum), which
describes Latin. Sentences introduced as comparisons, cognate lists, or ones
whose + sits entirely inside parentheses, are skipped. Without that guard the
wider scan put nuk back under ne, the exact defect this rule was written to fix.

105 genuine derivations recovered, including vdekje from vdes, takim from
takoj, ushqim from ushqej, mendim from mendoj and esell from e. Result: 3,029
families with 407 multi-member (was 3,082 and 381), 1,031 levels.

Verified: 188 tests across ten suites, the derivation parser suite now pinning
both directions of the late-sentence rule, family completeness with its
negative control, and the level view in Firefox showing e as and with no
example and esell in its own family.
